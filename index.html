UPDRAFT

Bounce up the endless sky tower. How high can you get?

How to play

  • You bounce by yourself. Land on a ledge and you spring up again.
  • Steer left and right: ← → or A / D, a gamepad, or hold the left or right half of the screen. Phones can use tilt too.
  • The edges wrap. Go off one side and you come back on the other.
  • No checkpoints. Miss and you fall onto whatever is below. Your score is the highest point you reached, so a fall only costs time.
  • One hit ends the run. Drones, lightning and space junk. You can squash a drone by landing on it from above.
  • It gets harder as you climb: smaller ledges, bigger gaps, drifting and crumbling ledges, fakes with a faint dotted underside, wind gusts (watch the arrow), ice, and springs for a big boost.
