How to play

  • You bounce by yourself. Just steer: ← → or A / D, a gamepad, or hold either side of the screen. The edges wrap.
  • Don't fall. The lava follows you up. Your score is the highest point you reach.
  • One hit ends the run: bats, rockfalls and wisps. Land on a bat to squash it.
  • Grab help: geysers and lava bubbles carry you up, a blue shell blocks one hit, mushrooms bounce you high.
  • Watch your step: cracked ledges break, and fakes have a broken glow.
  • Daily Climb: the same volcano for everyone today, with its own board. Race your ghost.
  • Others playing now show up live. Press ▶ on the board to watch a top climb.
  • Ash shows you around. Tilt steering, calm effects and more are in Settings.
